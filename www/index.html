How to use

Tap a part to add it. Drag a part to move it; legs snap into breadboard holes.
Drag from any hole or leg to draw a wire. Tap a wire, then drag its round ends to reconnect, or drag the small + dots to bend it.
Pinch or scroll to zoom; drag empty space to pan.
